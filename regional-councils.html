Regional Councils

Philippine Librarians Association, Inc.

Regular members of the Association are grouped into councils representing the different regions covering the entire Philippine archipelago.

Each Regional Council will conduct their respective business and affairs and shall perform such acts as shall advance librarianship and protect the rights and interests of its members, librarianship and the Association pursuant to the PLAI Constitution and By-Laws.
